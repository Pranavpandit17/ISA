Indore Software Association

About Us

Indore Software Association (ISA) is the definitive ecosystem for Indore's IT founders, CXOs, and technology leaders. We foster innovation, advocate on policy, and enable resource sharing to accelerate growth across the region's software industry.

Who we are

ISA brings together software product companies, IT services firms, and technology leaders from Indore under a single registered body. Through member meets, knowledge sessions, a member directory, and a bench exchange, we help our members hire better, sell better, and build better.

What we do

  • Convene founders and CXOs through curated events, roundtables, and annual conferences.
  • Represent the local software industry in conversations with government and academia.
  • Enable business and talent exchange between member organizations.
  • Share market insights, hiring trends, and best practices across the community.
